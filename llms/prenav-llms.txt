# PRENAV

> PRENAV, Inc. automates the visual inspection of large civil and industrial
> structures using off-the-shelf drones, photogrammetry and deep learning. Its
> PRENAV.XYZ web platform turns thousands of drone photographs into a 3D
> point-cloud digital twin and runs ML defect-detection models over it. A second
> line, Synthetic Imagery as a Service, procedurally renders annotated photoreal
> defect images to augment a customer's own AI training data.

## Machine-readable API surface

PRENAV publishes NO public API, NO developer portal, NO OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL
description, NO SDK on any public package registry, and NO MCP server. Probed
2026-08-26; every one of /openapi.json, /openapi.yaml, /swagger.json, /api-docs,
/llms.txt, /docs, /developers, /api and the full /.well-known/ discovery set
returned HTTP 404 on www.prenav.com. There is nothing here for an agent to call.
The correct action for an agent is to use the contact form.

## Products

- [PRENAV.XYZ](https://www.prenav.com/product-xyz): web-based automated visual
  inspection software. Upload a drone (or other camera/lidar) capture, get a 3D
  point-cloud digital twin navigable in the browser, exportable to common 3D
  formats for CAD. Deep-learning models detect cracking in concrete, spalling,
  exposed rebar, cracks in steel and rust; defects are marked, measured and
  included in an inspection report. Resolves features under 0.2mm.
- [Synthetic Imagery as a Service](https://www.prenav.com/synthetic-imagery):
  from a 3D model of a part plus a few example defect photos, PRENAV renders
  thousands of synthesized, annotated images per month matched to the customer's
  production camera specification and lighting environments.
- [Use cases](https://www.prenav.com/usecases): bridges, railroad bridges, cell
  towers, dams and spillways, aircraft, tunnels, power plant structures,
  transmission pylons, oil and gas facilities, building facades, wind turbine
  blades, tanker inspection.
